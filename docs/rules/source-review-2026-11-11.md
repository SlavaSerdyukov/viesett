Filename follows the requested milestone specification. **Actual review: 2026-11-12, Europe/Brussels.** No legal counsel approval or live certificate-registry authentication is claimed. Official public documentation was read; tests use synthetic local PDFs. No government endpoint is required by M3 runtime/CI.
