## Implemented boundaries from the review

Article 3.85 distinguishes authenticated basis-deed/co-ownership statutes from internal rules, and common-parts shares from expense-allocation criteria. M4 keeps these concepts separate and does not interpret enforceability or title rights from internal operational rules.

Article 3.86 §3 distinguishes periodic working-capital provision from reserves for non-periodic expenses. The reviewed reserve baseline is establishment no later than five years after provisional common-parts acceptance, annual contribution at least 5% of prior ordinary common charges, with a possible 4/5 decision not to constitute that mandatory reserve. M4 generates a REVIEW only with explicit relevant age/contribution/charge facts; a small reserve balance alone proves no breach. A documented waiver is a signal for review, not certification of vote validity. The statute's separate-account requirement was read but bank-account compliance checking is deferred.

Article 3.94 §1 supplies the information-package baseline before the relevant agreement/offer/promise. M4 uses its 15-day simple-request context and the eight product components described in [article-3-94.md](../co-ownership/article-3-94.md). Pending litigation includes amounts where supplied. General statutory applicability and completeness remain professional-review questions.

Article 3.94 §2 separately provides the registered notarial request, transfer-specific amounts, 30-day nonresponse framework and conditional allocation rules. Section 5 distinguishes working-capital adjustment from reserve funds remaining with the association. Article 3.95 addresses notarial withholding of outgoing-owner arrears, priorities and dispute procedure. These were reviewed to prevent presenting a proportional project estimate as a buyer debt; their full procedural rules are not automated.

Regulatory checks carry version 1.0.0, review date 2026-11-12, official source and Book 3 effective-from date. They do not claim historical transition-law analysis for older deeds/decisions. Cross-document mismatches and financial derivations are identified as product checks/arithmetic rather than statutory duties. No fabricated legal threshold, score or live legal-advice service exists. Later amendments require a new review and appropriate rule/version update.
