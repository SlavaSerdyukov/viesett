# Product boundaries

Viesett assists a residential buyer in Flanders with a source-backed property dossier. This foundation supports property workspaces, explicitly uploaded property documents, safety checks, structured evidence/findings, export/deletion and copyable document requests. It minimizes questions: ask only when the system cannot safely proceed. The first-upload representation is versioned rather than repeated for every file.

Viesett provides automated property and document information assistance. Legally significant decisions and binding documents should be reviewed with your Belgian notary or another qualified professional.

Viesett does not act as a licensed broker or represent buyers in negotiations, negotiate price or contract terms, submit binding offers, replace a Belgian notary, perform mortgage brokerage, evaluate creditworthiness, provide legal advice, sign documents or make legally binding decisions. The intended v1 journey stops before binding purchase workflow.

There is no mailbox access: no email-provider integration, OAuth mailbox credentials, inbound inbox, synchronization, correspondence parsing, sender/recipient graph or mail job. Users upload only relevant property files. Request drafts are plain text in English/Dutch, generated deterministically from document kinds, without a recipient. Generating text does not authorize communication. No backend action can send it; the user copies and sends it in their own channel.

Milestone 2 provides documented official GRAR identity and heritage facts, with a manual Waterinfo verification path. Milestone 3 adds local text-layer PDF processing for EPC, asbestos, electrical and soil certificates, page-backed DOCUMENTED evidence, explicit property association and versioned deterministic document requirements/findings. Unknown applicability remains conditional; insufficient text is OCR_REQUIRED. No overall risk score, purchase recommendation, valuation or legal sign-off is generated. Sources, exceptions and extraction limits are documented in the integration/rule reviews. No listing scraping, Woningpas private access, OCR service, paid AI, deep syndic analysis, mortgage comparison, notary integration, payment, frontend SPA or native app is implemented. No future AI processor may be silently enabled.

Privacy Notice acknowledgement is not GDPR consent. Upload attestation is a user representation under the Terms, not consent from third parties named in a document. No marketing consent exists. Legal bases are purpose-specific candidates requiring Belgian counsel review. The foundation is not a public service, legal compliance certification or formal DPIA approval.
