# Controlled private buyer evaluation

The purpose is to learn where buyers cannot understand or complete the existing dossier workflow. Use a small operator-provisioned cohort after [the operational checklist](checklist.md) is satisfied. Start with the labeled synthetic sample, then use entitled real material only under a reviewed private-data procedure. No buyers were studied in M6.

Ask the participant to attempt these tasks without a guided walkthrough:

1. Find a property by address and explain whether official resolution succeeded, needs a choice or is unavailable.
2. Create/open a private dossier; reload it and later log out.
3. Upload the relevant property PDFs without choosing their kind first.
4. Identify what is confirmed required, what may apply and what information is actually missing.
5. Explain the top finding in their own words and choose a practical next step.
6. Explain whether 100% dossier completeness means the property is safe.
7. Find the original source/page behind a claim; use the PDF fallback if necessary.
8. Explain a proportional works estimate and whether it establishes legal debt or buyer/seller responsibility.
9. Handle the optional deed-date question only if it appears and they know the date; explain why their answer stays unverified.
10. Find questions for the seller/syndic/notary, copy a question/request, and understand that Viesett sends nothing.
11. Prepare and print/save the notary dossier, checking sources and assumptions.
12. Delete a selected test source and explain the resulting dossier changes; demonstrate export/account deletion with the operator on a dedicated evaluation account.

The observer records task success, manually measured completion time, questions spoken aloud, misunderstandings, dead ends, unclear terms, unexpected clicks, missing information and trust concerns. Ask neutral follow-ups: “What does this mean to you?”, “What would you do next?”, “What made you trust or doubt this result?” Do not coach a participant toward a buy/no-buy answer or treat a successful click as comprehension.

Use coded participant references and minimal notes outside the product, for example a controlled gitignored `.local/private-beta-observations/` directory. Agree access, purpose and deletion with the operator. Do not build an interview database, analytics/replay SDK, automatic research export or screen recording. Any later recording/research-storage decision requires its own explicit privacy/product review.

## Authorized real-property procedure

Confirm that the tester is entitled to supply each relevant document. Exclude identity documents, eID/passports, unrelated correspondence and unnecessary banking/health/sensitive information. An upload representation is not blanket consent for other named people. Use the separate reviewed real-data environment, existing upload safety/acknowledgement and private storage; never copy files or exported source text into Git/issues/screenshots/test fixtures. Use opaque filenames in the local corpus manifest and report only aggregates.

Review wrong-property association, OCR/partial/unsupported results and missing applicability information without pretending the parser understood an entire document. Verify important details against the original and the buyer's notary/qualified professional. Demonstrate deletion and check pending physical cleanup/backup policy; remove evaluation sources/notes on the agreed schedule. The procedure supports controlled testing and does not replace privacy or legal review.

After the study, choose the next action from observed buyer confusion, authorized corpus compatibility and actual source reliability. Possible changes are hypotheses until those observations support them. No automatic next feature milestone, OCR/AI project or public launch follows M6.
