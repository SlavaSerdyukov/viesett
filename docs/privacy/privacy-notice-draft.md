# Viesett Privacy Notice — working draft

M6 adds an essential private browser session: a short-lived HttpOnly cookie, server-side session-secret hash/account/token linkage, expiry/revocation and a synchronizer token. The original private access token is exchanged without browser persistent storage. Local PDF viewing and user-directed print remain within the requested dossier purpose; no new processor, email, analytics or training purpose is introduced. Authorized-document compatibility evaluation stays local and reports only aggregates. See the [session design](../private-beta/sessions.md) and [real-material procedure](../private-beta/evaluation-guide.md).

This remains a draft clarification. Stored M4 policy texts/acknowledgements are preserved; they are not represented as a finalized M6 cookie/private-beta notice. Before actual real-data beta, review/publish the appropriate notice/version, session metadata retention, roles, transparency and operational procedures. No legal basis is changed or approved by this addition.

Viesett helps a buyer prepare a residential property due-diligence dossier in Flanders. We process a random account identifier, access-token hash/expiry, policy acknowledgements, property addresses, property-workspace metadata, uploaded property PDFs, safety results, structured property evidence with source locators, findings and copyable document requests. We do not require your birth date, telephone number, home address or national identifier to create a dossier. Account identity is separated from property evidence.

Files are processed only to analyse this property and create your Viesett buyer dossier. Upload only relevant property documents you are entitled to provide. Avoid identity documents, passports, Belgian eID, unrelated bank/health records, private correspondence and unnecessary sensitive information. We use bounded private storage and conservative checks, but automated detection is imperfect. Unsupported media is rejected, suspicious files are quarantined, and rejected malicious bytes are removed. Quarantined files do not enter evidence or AI workflows and are unavailable for ordinary download.

On request, safe READY PDFs are processed locally into page-level text and structured document facts. Complete derived text stays in private artifacts with the same dossier purpose and source retention criterion. Classification corrections, property association and versioned deterministic regulatory evaluations are recorded separately from document safety. Facts remain DOCUMENTED; uncertain/mismatched documents do not automatically affect substantive property findings. No new external processor, OCR SaaS or LLM is required. Source deletion removes derived artifacts and affected evaluation inputs. These rules assist review and make no binding legal decision.

Candidate legal bases differ by purpose and data subject: requested account/dossier service may rely on contract necessity for the user; handling relevant third-party property data and security may require assessed legitimate interests. Rights-request handling may involve applicable legal obligations where actually established. No blanket consent flag exists. The [legal-basis matrix](legal-basis-matrix.md) records necessity and unresolved decisions. Privacy Notice acknowledgement only records receipt of a version; Terms/upload representations do not obtain third-party consent. No marketing processing or implied marketing consent is implemented.

Documents may name sellers, agents, notaries, syndics, contractors or co-owners. A buyer's upload is not universal consent on their behalf. We limit copied property evidence, preserve provenance, and require an Article 14 assessment and a practical information/rights process before beta; see [third-party data](third-party-data.md). No correspondence is collected, and Viesett does not automatically contact anyone.

AI is optional and disabled in this foundation. No external AI processor is configured. Future reviewed interpretation receives only explicitly selected relevant sections of a ready document, with minimisation/redaction designed into extraction. It can add visibly inferred proposals, never overwrite official/documented evidence or give legal sign-off. **Customer documents, extracted content and dossiers are never used for product/model training by default.** This release forbids training use at both processor validation and database level. No data-reuse functionality exists.

This foundation uses local PostgreSQL and private filesystem objects and enables no external AI provider. When an authenticated owner requests official property resolution, the normalized address query and official identifiers are sent to GRAR / Digitaal Vlaanderen. Requested heritage enrichment sends only the selected official building area to Agentschap Onroerend Erfgoed. No account identity, token, uploaded file or extracted document content is sent to either source. The backend only constructs Waterinfo verification links; it does not retrieve or guess P/G-scores. Official/public observations, versions, query/refresh history, attribution and workspace links remain private, are included in export, and cascade on deletion. Historical evidence survives refresh within the dossier but is explicitly superseded when changed.

Contract necessity for the buyer's requested lookup and assessed legitimate interests for incidental third-party facts are candidates, subject to purpose/role review. Government data sources may act in their own controller capacity; calling one does not establish that it is a processor requiring a DPA. Determine roles, transparency and any applicable transfer safeguards before beta. Production public lookup requires an explicit operational review switch; it does not constitute GDPR consent or counsel approval. Before hosted operation, identify hosting/storage/support/scanning processors, review DPAs where applicable, access and locations, record recipients and assess transfer arrangements. Production refuses unreviewed enabled processors and those permitting training. A metadata record alone is not legal approval. The controller's final recipient/processor list and transfer notice remain open beta gates.

Active dossiers remain until user deletion or a reviewed retention policy applies. Users can archive workspaces; scheduled archive purge is disabled unless an operator configures a period. That value is a product configuration, not an asserted statutory requirement. Individual documents, workspaces and accounts can be deleted. The API makes deleted data inaccessible before retryable physical cleanup; a 503 pending-deletion response means cleanup is still outstanding. Cleanup reservations contain only opaque keys/timing and are removed after purge. Hosting backup expiration, recovery and security-log retention must be documented before beta, independently from dossier retention. No hidden exemption is used to retain deleted dossier content.

Authenticated metadata export includes account, policy versions acknowledged, workspaces, document metadata, evidence, findings and request drafts. It excludes bearer-token hashes and storage keys. Ready document binaries are downloaded separately with current authorization. Export is a practical foundation for user access/portability handling; it does not claim to satisfy every legal rights request automatically.

Access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and applicable portability requests will be handled through the designated privacy contact after proportionate identity/authority checks. Restriction and third-party requests currently require controlled operator handling; no automated third-party portal is supplied. Counsel must define deadlines, exceptions and identity checks before beta without over-collecting identity documents. Users may lodge a complaint with the [Belgian Data Protection Authority](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be/citizen).

Automated safety decisions and deterministic drafts assist dossier preparation. Viesett makes no binding purchase, credit, brokerage or legal decisions. There is no negotiation, offer submission, signing or automatic third-party communication. Review legally significant decisions and documents with a Belgian notary or other qualified professional.

Legal reference for counsel: [GDPR official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng). Controller roles, exact bases, Article 14 handling, retention/backups, processors/transfers, rights handling and DPIA scope remain unresolved; no public beta should rely on this unfinished notice.

M4 apartment review additionally derives VME/ACP organization identity, documented lot shares, separate working/reserve amounts, charge periods, meeting decisions, works, capital calls and property-related litigation categories. Versioned checks, proportional estimates and buyer questions remain private dossier data and do not decide legal payment responsibility. No new external processor or automatic communication is used. Recognized M4 derived artifacts retain normalized cited facts instead of full page text; the original PDF and earlier/M3/UNKNOWN artifacts remain private under the source retention criterion. Attendance lists, unrelated personal disputes, private contacts and individual banking details are not structured into M4 models. Incidental personal material in original or legacy text still requires the existing minimisation, rights and retention review. Prior notices and acknowledgements are preserved; upload attestations are unchanged.

## M5 product clarification — 2026-11-12

The first web interface presents the same private dossier purpose. Before a dossier is created, a requested address preview sends only the address/selected official identifiers to the existing official source; it creates no account, private workspace or persistent preview history. The server uses bounded ephemeral hashed network-peer buckets to limit abuse. Source operators may have their own processing practices, which still need role/transparency review. The start screen explains the external address lookup; final public transparency text remains a beta gate.

The optional local evaluation session uses a random, expiring bearer credential held only in page memory. It is disabled by default and forbidden in production. The browser loads a private PDF only when opened, through the authorized API into a temporary Blob URL. Dossier printing/copying is an explicit user action; the service sends nothing to a notary or mailbox. No analytics, replay, new processor, training use or external AI is introduced. Same-origin rendering and local print do not establish a new legal basis. Existing draft status, recipient/transfer/retention questions and public-launch requirements below remain unresolved.

This documentation clarification does not replace stored policy text or acknowledgements. Current persisted notice remains `2026-11-12-m4`; upload attestation is unchanged. A reviewed notice/version for hosted public preview and final browser authentication must be completed before beta.
