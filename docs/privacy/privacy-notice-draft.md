# Viesett Privacy Notice — working draft

**DRAFT - requires Belgian legal review before public launch.** Requires Belgian legal/privacy review before public beta. Version: 2026-11-11. This document is not approved by Belgian counsel and is not a claim of GDPR compliance.

Controller: **[legal entity and registered address to be designated]**. Privacy contact: **[contact channel to be designated before beta]**. Representative/DPO details, if applicable: **[assessment pending]**. Do not publish this placeholder as a completed notice.

Viesett helps a buyer prepare a residential property due-diligence dossier in Flanders. We process a random account identifier, access-token hash/expiry, policy acknowledgements, property addresses, property-workspace metadata, uploaded property PDFs, safety results, structured property evidence with source locators, findings and copyable document requests. We do not require your birth date, telephone number, home address or national identifier to create a dossier. Account identity is separated from property evidence.

Files are processed only to analyse this property and create your Viesett buyer dossier. Upload only relevant property documents you are entitled to provide. Avoid identity documents, passports, Belgian eID, unrelated bank/health records, private correspondence and unnecessary sensitive information. We use bounded private storage and conservative checks, but automated detection is imperfect. Unsupported media is rejected, suspicious files are quarantined, and rejected malicious bytes are removed. Quarantined files do not enter evidence or AI workflows and are unavailable for ordinary download.

Candidate legal bases differ by purpose and data subject: requested account/dossier service may rely on contract necessity for the user; handling relevant third-party property data and security may require assessed legitimate interests. Rights-request handling may involve applicable legal obligations where actually established. No blanket consent flag exists. The [legal-basis matrix](legal-basis-matrix.md) records necessity and unresolved decisions. Privacy Notice acknowledgement only records receipt of a version; Terms/upload representations do not obtain third-party consent. No marketing processing or implied marketing consent is implemented.

Documents may name sellers, agents, notaries, syndics, contractors or co-owners. A buyer's upload is not universal consent on their behalf. We limit copied property evidence, preserve provenance, and require an Article 14 assessment and a practical information/rights process before beta; see [third-party data](third-party-data.md). No correspondence is collected, and Viesett does not automatically contact anyone.

AI is optional and disabled in this foundation. No external AI processor is configured. Future reviewed interpretation receives only explicitly selected relevant sections of a ready document, with minimisation/redaction designed into extraction. It can add visibly inferred proposals, never overwrite official/documented evidence or give legal sign-off. **Customer documents, extracted content and dossiers are never used for product/model training by default.** This release forbids training use at both processor validation and database level. No data-reuse functionality exists.

This local foundation uses local PostgreSQL and private filesystem objects. It enables no external recipient, AI provider or international transfer. Before hosted operation, identify hosting/storage/support/scanning processors, review DPAs, access and locations, record recipients and assess transfer arrangements. Enabling a processor is an explicit operational decision; production refuses unreviewed processors and those permitting training. A metadata record alone is not legal approval. The controller's final processor list and transfer notice remain open beta gates.

Active dossiers remain until user deletion or a reviewed retention policy applies. Users can archive workspaces; scheduled archive purge is disabled unless an operator configures a period. That value is a product configuration, not an asserted statutory requirement. Individual documents, workspaces and accounts can be deleted. The API makes deleted data inaccessible before retryable physical cleanup; a 503 pending-deletion response means cleanup is still outstanding. Cleanup reservations contain only opaque keys/timing and are removed after purge. Hosting backup expiration, recovery and security-log retention must be documented before beta, independently from dossier retention. No hidden exemption is used to retain deleted dossier content.

Authenticated metadata export includes account, policy versions acknowledged, workspaces, document metadata, evidence, findings and request drafts. It excludes bearer-token hashes and storage keys. Ready document binaries are downloaded separately with current authorization. Export is a practical foundation for user access/portability handling; it does not claim to satisfy every legal rights request automatically.

Access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and applicable portability requests will be handled through the designated privacy contact after proportionate identity/authority checks. Restriction and third-party requests currently require controlled operator handling; no automated third-party portal is supplied. Counsel must define deadlines, exceptions and identity checks before beta without over-collecting identity documents. Users may lodge a complaint with the [Belgian Data Protection Authority](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be/citizen).

Automated safety decisions and deterministic drafts assist dossier preparation. Viesett makes no binding purchase, credit, brokerage or legal decisions. There is no negotiation, offer submission, signing or automatic third-party communication. Review legally significant decisions and documents with a Belgian notary or other qualified professional.

Legal reference for counsel: [GDPR official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng). Controller roles, exact bases, Article 14 handling, retention/backups, processors/transfers, rights handling and DPIA scope remain unresolved; no public beta should rely on this unfinished notice.
