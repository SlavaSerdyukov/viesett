# Third-party data handling

**DRAFT - requires Belgian legal/privacy review before public beta.** A buyer's upload or Terms representation does not provide consent for a seller, agent, notary, syndic, contractor, co-owner or anyone else named in a document.

Only the property-relevant file should be uploaded. Identity documents, National Register numbers, Belgian eID, passports, health, political/religious/sexual-life, biometric/genetic data and unrelated banking or correspondence are unnecessary. These are not supported document kinds or property identity fields. The conservative gate quarantines clear high-risk markers; it is imperfect, especially for images, compressed and encrypted PDFs. No claim of comprehensive detection is made. Quarantined/rejected documents do not enter evidence or AI workflows.

Property evidence stores typed facts and locators to a particular document/page/section or source identifier. Avoid copying names, contact addresses or identifiers into evidence unless a future reviewed purpose requires them. Keep account identity separate, and never merge unrelated dossiers or deduplicate across users. Selected content for future AI should be minimised/redacted before the interpretation port is invoked. There is no archive-forwarding, training or data-reuse pathway.

Before beta, counsel must assess Article 14 information obligations for data obtained through buyers, including timing, scope, feasible notice mechanisms and any exception that could actually apply. Do not assume public availability, buyer attestation, effort or commercial inconvenience automatically removes transparency duties. See the [official GDPR, Article 14](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng). The current software does not contact third parties; any required notice procedure must be designed and operated explicitly without quietly introducing mailbox access.

On a third-party access, correction, deletion or restriction request, an authorized operator should verify identity/authority proportionately, locate the relevant source and workspace, assess competing rights and the lawful response, and record minimal case metadata in a controlled operational process. Do not disclose the buyer's entire dossier simply because a person appears in one file. Do not collect a passport by default to handle a routine request. There is no automated legal determination or third-party portal.

Restriction is not implemented as a buyer-facing endpoint. Before beta, the operator must be able to suspend affected document processing/access while counsel resolves a case; until that workflow exists, do not open the service to public third-party material. Deletion services already remove a scoped file, linked evidence/findings and invalidated drafts, or the full workspace/account; physical failures retain an opaque purge task and report pending cleanup. Review backup and processor deletion completion separately.

Open questions: controller/processor allocation between Viesett and buyer; necessity and legitimate-interest balancing; incidental special-category/identifier handling; Article 14 procedure and exceptions; property-document access/retention restrictions; rights verification/redaction; restriction workflows; applicable deadlines; processor locations/support access; and incident notification responsibilities. No legal bases, exceptions or formal approval have been invented.

## M4 scoped addition — 2026-11-12

M4 does not create owner attendance arrays or named litigation/debt profiles. Seller arrears are extracted only under explicit seller/lot labels; unrelated owners' personal debt is not a field. Corporate entity identity and controlled litigation/work categories support the property purpose. Recognized M4 artifacts retain normalized cited facts instead of a second full-text attendance/banking copy. Original uploads and earlier/M3/UNKNOWN artifacts remain private and may still contain incidental personal information; do not represent this as source redaction. Dossier export omits raw text and storage keys; source deletion erases affected derived snapshots and recomputes remaining facts. Notice 2026-11-12-m4 preserves old notices/acknowledgements and changes no upload consent semantics.

## M5 scoped addition — 2026-11-12

Anonymous preview cannot retrieve private documents, co-ownership material, evidence or findings. Only requested official property fields are projected; address/official-ID transmission still needs the existing role and transparency review. Private dossier pages preserve owner-only access and evidence authority; no named parties are added merely to support the interface. Clicking a source PDF uses authorized local viewing, and preparing for a notary opens a print view without contacting the notary. Copy/print/download is user-directed and should not be described as an automated rights-disclosure mechanism. Existing Article 14, correction, restriction, redaction and launch questions remain open.
