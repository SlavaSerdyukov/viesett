11. **Basis deed:** bounded building/entity, private lot/designation, attached cellar/garage/parking identifier and common-parts share. Multiple conflicting lot/share values are not selected arbitrarily. No general deed interpretation or automatic title-right inference.
12. **Shares/allocation:** integer numerator/denominator and printed rational representation are preserved. General ownership and explicit expense keys remain distinct. A matching project's explicit key takes precedence; general ownership is used only with an explicit applicability statement. Unknown/conflicting allocation blocks a precise result.
13. **AGM:** typed meeting/date/type, agenda number, normalized topic/decision, explicit decision status, approved versus estimated budget, vote, funding, project allocation, call total and execution date with evidence. Normalized controlled decision text replaces arbitrary personal paragraphs. Corporate contractor extraction and unrestricted decision text are deliberately unsupported. Proposed/rejected/postponed/information-only are distinct from approved.

15. **Capital calls:** reference, project link, exact total and explicit lot amount, due/effective dates, supported controlled purpose/fund type and citations. Legal buyer/seller payment responsibility remains unknown.
16. **Periodic charges:** explicit period, lot, ordinary/extraordinary charges, reserve/working contributions and due/credit amounts. No full ledger reconciliation, VAT audit or arbitrary table parsing.
17. **Balance sheet:** explicit date, approval date, identifier, cash/bank total, separate working/reserve amounts, aggregate receivables/payables. Latestness is documentary and may need confirmation. No statutory accounting audit.
18. **Funds/reserve rule:** separate dated observations and current building amounts; lot-specific amounts preserve lot context and do not become building balances. Different dates/lots/currencies do not create automatic disagreements. Vote validity and account compliance are not certified.
